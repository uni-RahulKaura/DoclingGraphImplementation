SUPPLY AGREEMENT AMENDMENT

<a id='0002'></a>

This Amendment, effective March 14, 2021, amends the Supply Agreement between
**Northwind Instruments, Inc.** ("Northwind") and **Baltic Components AG** ("Supplier")
which became effective June 1, 2016, as amended ("Agreement").

<a id='0003'></a>

## 1. TERM

The first sentence of Section 8A is deleted in its entirety and replaced with the
following: This Agreement will continue in effect until March 31, 2024.

<a id='0004'></a>

## 2. PRICING AND PAYMENT

Supplier shall invoice Northwind monthly. Northwind will pay each undisputed invoice
net 45 days from receipt. Unit pricing is fixed at USD 12,400.00 per assembly for the
duration of this Amendment.

<a id='0005'></a>

## 3. OBLIGATIONS OF SUPPLIER

Supplier shall maintain the quality management system described in Attachment A.
Supplier must notify Northwind in writing at least ninety (90) days prior to any change
in manufacturing location.

<a id='0006'></a>

## 4. RESTRICTIONS

Supplier may not subcontract any portion of the Services without the prior written
consent of Northwind. Supplier shall not use the Northwind name in any publicity
without prior written approval.

<a id='0007'></a>
